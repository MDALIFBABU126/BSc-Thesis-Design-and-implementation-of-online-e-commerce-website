# BSc-Thesis-Design-and-implementation-of-online-e-commerce-website
1. Main Objective

The main objective of this thesis is to design and develop an online E-Commerce website where customers can browse products, select their desired items, and purchase them from anywhere through the internet.

The main goals are:

- To provide an easy and user-friendly online shopping experience.
- To allow customers to register and log in.
- To enable customers to browse and purchase products.
- To provide a shopping cart system.
- To integrate an online payment system.
- To allow administrators to manage products, customers, and orders.

2. Problem Statement

The thesis identifies several common problems in online shopping:

1. Refund Problems: Customers may face difficulties when requesting refunds for cancelled or returned orders.
2. Price Drop Problems: Product prices may decrease immediately after a customer places an order.
3. Customer Service Problems: Customer support may not always provide effective solutions.
4. Payment Security: Customers may have concerns about the security of their personal and financial information.

The proposed system aims to improve the online shopping experience through better management, customer support, and payment processes.

4. Main System Modules

The website consists of two major interfaces.

A. Customer Interface

Customers can:

- Register a new account.
- Log in to their accounts.
- View available products.
- Add products to the shopping cart.
- Update or remove products from the cart.
- Manage their accounts.
- View order history.
- Complete online purchases.

B. Admin Interface

Administrators can:

- Register and log in.
- Add new products.
- Update product information.
- Delete products.
- Manage customer information.
- Manage customer orders.
- Control website content.

5. Database Design

The project uses MySQL as its database management system.

The database stores and manages important information, including customer details, product information, shopping cart data, and order records.

The main database tables mentioned in the thesis include:

- Admins
- Brands
- Cart
- Categories
- Clients
- Orders
- Installments
- Items

The main purpose of the database is to store information properly and maintain communication between the website and its users.

6. System Implementation

The implementation phase focuses on developing the main functionalities of the website.

The major implemented features include:

1. Customer Registration System
2. Customer Login System
3. Admin Registration and Login
4. Product Display System
5. Shopping Cart Management
6. Customer Account Management
7. PayPal Payment Integration
8. Admin Management System

PHP is used to connect the website with the MySQL database and manage dynamic information.

7. Payment System

The website integrates PayPal as its online payment gateway.

The payment process follows these steps:

Select Product

Add to Shopping Cart

Customer Login

Checkout

PayPal Payment

Order Confirmation

After successful payment, the customer receives purchase confirmation and relevant order information.

8. Testing and Results

Different testing procedures were performed to verify the functionality of the website.

The testing results showed that:

- Customers could successfully register and log in.
- Products could be displayed correctly.
- Customers could add products to their shopping carts.
- Products could be updated or removed from the cart.
- Customers could proceed to PayPal checkout.
- The shopping cart became empty after completing checkout.
- Administrators could manage website content and orders.

These results demonstrate that the main functions of the system were working as expected.

9. Conclusion

This thesis presents the design and implementation of a functional online E-Commerce website.

The system provides separate interfaces for customers and administrators. Customers can browse products, manage their shopping carts, and make online payments. Administrators can manage products, customers, and orders.

By using PHP, MySQL, HTML5, and CSS3, the project develops a user-friendly online shopping platform.

The main achievement of this project is providing a convenient and organized online shopping system that makes purchasing products easier for customers and simplifies store management for administrators.
